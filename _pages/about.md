Hi! My name is Seo-young Lee and I am a postdoctoral researcher at Drexel University. I worked as a Senior User Experience (UX) Manager at Samsung Life Insurance. Also, I completed Ph.D. in Human-Centered Design and B.A. in Communication from Cornell University.

My research focuses on how technology shapes human collaboration, such as decision-making in design and healthcare contexts. I also study how human-centric technology can empower underserved populations. I approach problems by deeply understanding how people understand and perceive technology in their surrounding environment, focusing on UX design and psychology.
