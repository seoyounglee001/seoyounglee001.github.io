Hi! My name is Seo-young Lee and I am a postdoctoral researcher at **A.J. Drexel Autism Institute**. I worked as a **Senior User Experience (UX) Manager** at Samsung Life Insurance. Also, I completed Ph.D. in Human-Centered Design and B.A. in Communication from **Cornell University**. 

My research focuses on **how technology shapes human collaboration**, such as **decision-making** in **healthcare and design**. I also study how **human-centric technology** can empower **underserved populations**. I approach problems by deeply understanding how people understand and perceive technology in their **surrounding environment**, focusing on **UX design** and **psychology**. For research collaborations or other academic opportunities, please feel free to reach out to me at **anneseoyoung.lee@gmail.com.**
