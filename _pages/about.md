Hi! My name is Seo-young Lee and I am a postdoctoral researcher at Drexel University. I worked as a Senior User Experience (UX) Manager at Samsung Life Insurance. Also, I completed Ph.D. in Human-Centered Design and B.A. in Communication from Cornell University. My research focuses on <span style="color: #4169E1;"> how technology shapes human collaboration </span>, such as <span style="color: #4169E1;">decision-making</span> in <span style="color: #4169E1;">healthcare</span> contexts. I also study <span style="color: #4169E1;">how human-centric technology can empower underserved populations</span>. I approach problems by deeply understanding how people understand and perceive technology in their <span style="color: #4169E1;">surrounding environment</span>, focusing on <span style="color: #4169E1;"> UX design and psychology</span>.
